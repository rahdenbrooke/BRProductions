Meet Our Cast and Crew

Get to know the talented individuals who make these short films possible. Each person on this list has brought their unique skills and passions to each of these projects, and has made a significant impact on the outcome of the film. These people are some of my closest friends, and I am so grateful to have gotten to work with them on these projects.

Cast

Grace Foley

Grace has been a crucial part of both films, and has done an amazing job bringing her characters to life. She has a natural talent for acting, and has displayed her wide range with numerous roles in both her theater and film performances. None of these projects would have been possible without her.

Roles: Hannah in Someone Like Her Leah in Bowstring

Taylor Kohring

Taylor has shown her depth and autheticity in both films, delivering powerful performances that have left the audience with a lasting impression. Her dedication to theater and film is evident in her work, and she continues to amaze with her talent.

Roles: Cassidy in Someone Like Her Natalie in Bowstring

Archie Ladage

Archie, though not someone who has a passion for acting, has done an incredible job in both films. His natural charisma and presence on screen have made him a standout in both projects. He is also a great help to the crew and a close friend outisde of filming.

Roles: Studio Coworker in Someone Like Her Riley in Bowstring

Sarah Lanemann

Sarah delivered a captivating and antagonistic performance in Someone Like Her, and continued to help with camerawork and production in Bowstring. Her help behind the scenes and on screen has been of utmost importance for the success of both films.

Roles: Alice in Someone Like Her Assistant Cameraperson in Bowstring

Brooke Rahden

Brooke Rahden (yours truly): writer, director, actor, editor, and producer of both films. Brooke has always had a passion for storytelling, and has been writing and wanting to create films since a young age. These projects have been a labor of love, dedication, and hard work from everyone invovled.

Roles: Creator of Someone Like Her and Bowstring Quinn in Bowstring

Crew

Sienna McGilvery

Sienna has been an incredible help behind the scenes as the composer for Bowstring's score. She has dedicated a large portion of her life to appreciating and understanding music, which is evident through her work. Alongside her musical contributions, Sienna was also Brooke's personal cheerleader and support system throughout both filmmaking processes.

Roles: Composer for Bowstring

Taylor Rahden

Taylor has demonstrated her utmost patience while working with Brooke on these projects. As Brooke's twin sister, she has continuted to encourage and support each project from their very early stages. Taylor has been a huge help behind the scenes, testing and helping Brooke with various aspects of filming.

Roles: Assistant Cameraperson for Someone Like Her Assistant Cameraperson for Bowstring
